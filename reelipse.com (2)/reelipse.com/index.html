Frequently asked questions

What is Reelipse?+

Reelipse is a one-stop guide to every film and TV show on TMDB, well over a million titles. For each one you get a quick verdict, cast, runtime, age rating, whether there is a post-credits scene, where to stream it and what to watch next. Shows also get a guide to every season and episode.

How often is trending updated?+

Trending, in cinemas and coming soon refresh every half hour from TMDB, so the home page always shows what people are watching right now.

Where do the verdicts come from?+

From TMDB user scores. Absolute Cinema is 7.9 and above, Worth the Hype 7 to 7.8, Popcorn Flick 6 to 6.9, Snoozefest below 6. Films need at least 40 ratings before they get a verdict.

Does Reelipse cover Indian movies?+

Yes. Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Bengali and Marathi films sit alongside Hollywood, Korean and Japanese cinema, with the same verdicts and recommendations.

Is Reelipse free?+

Yes. No account is needed. Your watchlist is stored privately in your own browser.
